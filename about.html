About Paws & Hearts

We're dedicated to finding loving homes for pets in need and supporting animal welfare in our community.

Our Mission

Finding Forever Homes

At PawsHome, our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and surrendered animals. We believe every pet deserves a loving home and proper care. Through education, advocacy, and community engagement, we strive to end pet homelessness and promote responsible pet ownership.

Founded in 2019, we've helped over 3,000 animals find their forever homes. Our no-kill policy ensures every animal gets the time they need to find the perfect match.

Our Facilities

We provide a safe, clean, and loving environment for all animals in our care.

Main Shelter

Our 10,000 sq ft main building houses up to 50 dogs and 75 cats in comfortable, spacious accommodations designed to reduce stress and promote well-being.

Veterinary Clinic

Our on-site veterinary clinic provides comprehensive medical care, including spay/neuter services, vaccinations, and emergency treatment.

Play & Exercise Areas

Multiple indoor and outdoor play areas give our animals plenty of space to exercise, socialize, and show their true personalities to potential adopters.
